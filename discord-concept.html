WHAT'S WRONG WITH MOST FiveM MC DISCORDS?

  • ✕Channel creep: 60+ channels, 4 "general" chats, nobody knows where to post.
  • ✕No funnel: Recruitment DMs get lost, prospects never see bylaws.
  • ✕Flat roles: Everyone @everyone, Church leaks, no need-to-know.
  • ✕Voice chaos: 12 empty VCs, no stage for Church or debrief.
  • ✕Brand drift: Looks like a gaming server, not a 1%er clubhouse.

↳ This rework fixes all of it — scroll down to test-drive the new layout.

⛔ Current (Typical Messy MC)

  • 12-15 categories, inconsistent icons & ordering
  • #general, #chat, #main, #lounge all active — duplicate conversations
  • #rules + #bylaws + #info spread across 3 categories
  • Prospect channels mixed with Patched channels — leaks & confusion
  • No onboarding; new joins land in #general and get ignored
  • Everyone can @everyone, announcement spam

✦ Refined (This Proposal)

  • 7 tight categories, ordered by member journey: Welcome → Prospect → Clubhouse → Church
  • Single source of truth: #welcome, #bylaws, #announcements at top, read-only
  • Hangaround → Prospect → Patched funnel with auto-roles & tickets
  • Need-to-know perms: Church, War Room, Treasury invisible to prospects
  • Onboarding: verify → rules → faction-select → introduction ticket
  • Only High Command can @everyone; announcements are clean & pinned

ONBOARDING FUNNEL

New joiners go from cold invite → vetted Prospect in under 3 minutes, no admin DM spam.

1 — Landing & Verify

Captcha / Wick + read #welcome. Click ✅ to unlock #rules. Auto-role @Unverified → @Hangaround.

2 — Read Rules & Bylaws

#rules (5 Directives) + #bylaws + #chain-of-command are read-only. React 📜 to acknowledge.

3 — Introduction Ticket

Button in #recruitment → Ticket bot → “Who are you? Age? RP exp? Why Hounds?” → Staff triage.

4 — Hangaround Lounge

Approved = access to #hangaround-lounge (IC chat) + #prospect-info. Observe rides for 7 days.

5 — Prospect Patch

Vote in #church-votes → @Prospect + prospect-hall, mentor assigned, tasks tracked.

Why funnel? Stops randoms flooding #general, filters time-wasters, and gives prospects a clear ladder: Hangaround → Prospect → Patched — exactly like your IRL 1%er lineage.
