RECRUITMENT

Thinking about wearing the patch? Here's what it takes.

Requirements

  • Age 18+ and 16+ in character
  • Own a motorcycle and know how to ride it
  • No recent criminal record in the state (in character)
  • Able to attend at least 2 club runs per week
  • Willing to start as a Prospect
  • Voice chat required, no combat logging
