Standing Orders

  • 01
    Callsign is your only rank.All callsigns are equal. Leave your rank at the door.
  • 02
    No spectators.Participation is required. Make a decision and commit.
  • 03
    You receive what you give.Respect all callsigns. Challenge ideas, not people.
  • 04
    Have a reason.You don't need to be right. You need to be able to justify it.
  • 05
    The timer is law.When time expires, orders stand as written. Plan accordingly.
  • 06
    Debrief is for learning, not relitigating.Analyse what happened. Don't defend what you did.
